**OFFICE OF SIR REGINALD HARCOURT**  
*Private and Confidential*

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am directed by Sir Reginald Harcourt to request the favour of your attendance at the **Ritz Hotel, Cairo, on Thursday, 18 October 1934**, in connection with a private expedition which he has undertaken to finance.

The object of the expedition is to recover an antiquity long believed lost. We have lately received word that other interested parties are making enquiries as to its whereabouts. In view of their activity, Sir Reginald has found it necessary to look beyond the contractors ordinarily employed on such undertakings and to approach persons of recognised ability in their respective fields. He would be glad of an opportunity to discuss whether your particular experience might be placed at the expedition’s disposal.

The particulars of your journey, together with the travel arrangements made on your behalf, are enclosed. On arrival at the hotel, please present this letter at the reception desk. Interviews will be held during the afternoon in the hotel’s **Conference Room**, and the desk will advise you of the hour appointed for yours.

Those selected following interview will be invited to dine with Sir Reginald in the hotel’s private dining room at **eight o’clock that evening**. Sir Reginald will then explain the object of the expedition in full and lay before his guests the arrangements for its departure.

I should be obliged if you would confirm by return whether you are able to attend. In the meantime, Sir Reginald asks that you treat this correspondence and its enclosures as strictly private.

I am, yours faithfully,

*Eleanor Marsh*

Eleanor Marsh  
Private Secretary to Sir Reginald Harcourt
