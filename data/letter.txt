> 14th of November, 1934

# Dear Friend,

This is where your opening letter goes: the hook, the job offer, the mysterious summons. Whatever gets your players leaning in.

Keep it to a page or so. Short paragraphs read well on screen. A line beginning with # becomes a heading, and a line beginning with > is set to the right, like a date.

Choose your file. Learn your companions' names. We leave at dawn.

Yours in confidence,

The Patron
