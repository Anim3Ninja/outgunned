EGYPTIAN EXPEDITION — PERSONNEL ASSESSMENT
Office of Sir Reginald Harcourt · Private and Confidential · 1934

Candidate:  Miss Evelyn “Evie” Sullivan
Age and nationality:  27, American
Present occupation:  Investigative newspaper reporter, Chicago
Proposed appointment:  Expedition Correspondent and Researcher

### Experience and requirements

- [X] Reporting. Progressed from society coverage to investigations involving organised crime and public corruption.
- [X] Access and interviews. At ease in formal company and able to put a nervous witness at ease.
- [X] Verification. Checks accounts against records and follows up small discrepancies that others overlook.
- [X] Confidential sources. Takes care to protect informants and can handle information given in confidence.
- [~] Expedition experience. Accustomed to reporting under pressure. No extended overseas field assignment appears in her record.
- [ ] Publication terms. Timing of dispatches and limits on what may be published during the expedition remain to be agreed.

### Personal assessment

Miss Sullivan is personable, persistent and attentive to detail. She has a knack for encouraging people to talk freely and remembers more of a conversation than her manner might suggest. A promising lead can take her well beyond the assignment she was given. She will press for an explanation if asked to leave a fact out of her account.

### Recommendation

SUITABLE — OFFER APPOINTMENT SUBJECT TO PUBLICATION TERMS

Engage Miss Sullivan to record the expedition and make enquiries where a reporter’s access would be useful. Agree when she may send dispatches and how long the route and location of any finds must remain confidential.
