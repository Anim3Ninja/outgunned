EGYPTIAN EXPEDITION — PERSONNEL ASSESSMENT
Office of Sir Reginald Harcourt · Private and Confidential · 1934

Candidate:  Major Charles Edmund Hargreaves, DSO (Retired)
Age and nationality:  60, British
Present occupation:  Retired British Army officer, Royal Warwickshire Regiment
Proposed appointment:  Expedition Security and Field Command

### Experience and requirements

- [X] Command experience. Served in South Africa and on the Western Front. Awarded the Distinguished Service Order for leadership in the field.
- [X] Field organisation. Experienced in reading terrain, assigning duties and making arrangements for emergencies.
- [X] Briefing and training. Held post-war staff and training appointments. Can give clear instructions under pressure.
- [~] Recent field experience. Retired in 1923. His judgement in the field has not been tested in recent years.
- [ ] Local knowledge. No service in Egypt appears in the available record. The expedition guide should advise on local conditions.

### Personal assessment

Major Hargreaves is disciplined, decisive and accustomed to taking responsibility for others. He takes preparation seriously and can be expected to give clear direction in an emergency. His instinct is to deal with trouble openly and promptly. He may expect civilian specialists to accept orders as readily as soldiers did. Settle the limits of his authority before departure.

### Recommendation

SUITABLE — OFFER APPOINTMENT FOR FIELD SECURITY

Engage Major Hargreaves to organise camp security and direct the party’s immediate response to danger. Leave routine decisions on routes and fieldwork with the specialists responsible for them.
