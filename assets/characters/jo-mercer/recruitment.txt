EGYPTIAN EXPEDITION — PERSONNEL ASSESSMENT
Office of Sir Reginald Harcourt · Private and Confidential · 1934

Candidate:  Miss Josephine “Jo” Mercer
Age and nationality:  33, American
Present occupation:  Freight driver and motor mechanic, California
Proposed appointment:  Motor Transport and Mechanical Support

### Experience and requirements

- [X] Heavy freight. Experienced in carrying loaded lorries through Death Valley and over poor roads.
- [X] Wartime transport. Served in the North African campaigns during the Great War. Experienced in moving people and supplies through desert country.
- [X] Local knowledge. Familiar with Egyptian desert travel and local conditions. Knows what to allow for when planning long journeys in extreme heat.
- [~] Local dialects. Has a working knowledge of Egyptian Arabic and some familiarity with local variations. Can ask directions, arrange supplies and discuss common faults with drivers and mechanics.
- [X] Vehicle maintenance. Services her own lorry and can diagnose faults in the field.
- [X] Roadside repairs. Resourceful when parts and workshop equipment are scarce.

### Personal assessment

Miss Mercer is practical, quick to act and plain-spoken about the condition of a vehicle. She expects to be heard on mechanical questions and can be sharp with anyone who gives her instructions without first looking at the fault. She’s confident taking a lorry through difficult ground and is inclined to keep going when a more cautious driver would stop.

### Recommendation

SUITABLE — OFFER SPECIALIST APPOINTMENT

Engage Miss Mercer to drive the expedition’s principal freight vehicle and oversee the condition of its motor transport. Consult her when planning desert journeys and give her authority to take an unsafe vehicle out of service.
