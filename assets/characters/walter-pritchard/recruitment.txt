EGYPTIAN EXPEDITION — PERSONNEL ASSESSMENT
Office of Sir Reginald Harcourt · Private and Confidential · 1934

Candidate:  Mr Walter Pritchard
Age and nationality:  26, American
Present occupation:  Civilian pilot, air racer and aircraft designer
Proposed appointment:  Expedition Pilot and Aerial Reconnaissance

### Experience and requirements

- [X] Professional flying. Holds a United States Transport Pilot’s Licence, renewed in February 1934.

- [X] Immediate availability. In Cairo for the international air race around the Pyramids of Giza and available at short notice.
- [~] Aerial survey. Appears capable of undertaking observation flights. Previous survey work is unconfirmed.
- [ ] References. Full enquiries could not be completed in the time available.
- [ ] Transport aircraft. A suitable machine for passengers and freight has yet to be secured.

### Personal assessment

Mr Pritchard is a last-minute candidate. He came under consideration solely because the air race brought him to Cairo just as the expedition was preparing to depart. The office has had time to examine his flying credentials and press record. His suitability for expedition work cannot be vouched for on the basis of these hurried enquiries.

He appears quick-thinking and technically capable. He enjoys an audience and has a reputation for taking chances when people are watching. Agree the purpose and limits of each flight in advance. Publicity arrangements should be settled separately.

### Recommendation

PROVISIONAL — OFFER APPOINTMENT ON A TRIAL BASIS

Engage Mr Pritchard initially for aerial observation. Review his performance before entrusting him with passengers or finds, and secure a suitable aircraft for that work. The decision on whether a flight can safely proceed rests with the pilot.
