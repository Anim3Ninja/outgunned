Office of Sir Reginald Harcourt · Private and Confidential · 1934

Candidate:  Mr Arthur Blackwood
Age and nationality:  Approximately 43, British
Present occupation:  Private enquiry agent, London
Proposed appointment:  Expedition Investigator

### Experience and requirements

- [X] Police experience. Approximately fifteen years investigating serious crime in London. Former Detective Inspector at Scotland Yard.
- [X] Witness enquiries. Patient with reluctant witnesses. Recognises discrepancies and follows them up without losing the confidence of the person being questioned.
- [X] Corroboration. Can reconstruct the order of events from statements, records and physical evidence.
- [X] Observation. Experienced in watching a person or premises discreetly and keeping a useful record of what he sees.
- [~] Strenuous fieldwork. An old shoulder wound limits heavy or sustained physical work.
- [ ] Enquiries in Egypt. No previous local work is on record. Arrange introductions where official assistance is required.

### Personal assessment

Mr Blackwood is methodical, dry in manner and difficult to hurry past an inconsistency. He makes a clear distinction between what a witness saw and what the witness has since been told. He is prepared to question a client’s account as closely as anyone else’s. Give him access to the original records wherever possible.

### Recommendation

SUITABLE — OFFER SPECIALIST APPOINTMENT

Retain Mr Blackwood to establish when the Stone of the Nile was last reliably seen, trace who had access to it and report which accounts withstand examination.
