EGYPTIAN EXPEDITION — PERSONNEL ASSESSMENT
Office of Sir Reginald Harcourt · Private and Confidential · 1934

Candidate:  Miss Sophie Whitfield
Age and nationality:  29, British
Present occupation:  Archival researcher and manuscript conservator
Proposed appointment:  Manuscript and Inscriptions Specialist

### Experience and requirements

- [X] Ancient Egyptian texts. Can read inscriptions and compare incomplete passages with surviving records.
- [X] Document conservation. Experienced in handling fragile papers and recovering faded writing. Restored and translated the papyrus presented at the British Museum in 1932.
- [X] Archival research. Finds obscure records and traces discrepancies between accounts.
- [X] Documentation. Keeps careful transcripts and indicates where a reading is uncertain.
- [X] Preparation. Brings the tools and reference materials required for her work.
- [~] Expedition experience. Extensive work in British collections. Limited field experience.

### Personal assessment

Miss Whitfield is patient, precise and willing to question a conclusion when the evidence warrants it. She is accustomed to working on research teams and can explain a difficult reading without making a disagreement personal.

She may persist with a problem longer than the timetable allows before asking for help. Allow her sufficient time to examine fragile material and ask for a preliminary opinion if an immediate decision is required.

### Recommendation

SUITABLE — OFFER SPECIALIST APPOINTMENT

Engage Miss Whitfield to examine, preserve and interpret any written material the expedition recovers. Her findings should be recorded under her own name.
