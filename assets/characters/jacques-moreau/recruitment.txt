EGYPTIAN EXPEDITION — PERSONNEL ASSESSMENT
Office of Sir Reginald Harcourt · Private and Confidential · 1934

Candidate:  Captain Jacques Moreau
Age and nationality:  35, French
Present occupation:  Coastal cargo captain, based in Marseille
Proposed appointment:  Maritime Transport and Logistics

### Experience and requirements

- [X] Coastal freight. Owns and commands a small cargo vessel. Experienced in arranging shipments between Mediterranean ports.
- [X] Alexandria contacts. Knows the waterfront and can arrange labour, storage and onward carriage.
- [X] Procurement. Can locate vehicles, fuel and supplies at short notice.
- [X] Negotiation. Comfortable dealing with merchants, officials and crews. Resourceful when an arrangement falls through.
- [~] Commercial references. Reports from Marseille raise questions about undeclared cargo. The police inquiry resulted in no prosecution or conviction.
- [ ] Inland logistics. Experience beyond the towns and ports is unconfirmed.

### Personal assessment

Captain Moreau is personable, quick to grasp what a job requires and accustomed to making his own arrangements. His contacts could save the expedition considerable time on arrival. He is likely to want room to exercise his judgement, particularly when dealing with people he knows personally.

Obtain prices and delivery terms before committing funds. Any cargo accepted on the expedition’s behalf should be entered on its own inventory.

### Recommendation

SUITABLE — OFFER APPOINTMENT WITH FINANCIAL OVERSIGHT

Engage Captain Moreau to arrange receipt of freight in Alexandria and secure local transport and supplies. Give him discretion over whom he approaches, with expenditure subject to approval.
