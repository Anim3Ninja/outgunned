EGYPTIAN EXPEDITION — PERSONNEL ASSESSMENT
Office of Sir Reginald Harcourt · Private and Confidential · 1934

Candidate:  The Honourable Edward Pembroke
Age and nationality:  33, British
Present occupation:  Lecturer in Egyptology, Oxford
Proposed appointment:  Egyptologist and Academic Adviser

### Experience and requirements

- [X] Egyptian history and antiquities. Can identify finds and explain their significance to the expedition.
- [X] Inscriptions. Experienced in interpreting ancient Egyptian inscriptions, particularly those connected with burial and religious practice.
- [X] Excavation and recording. Worked on excavations in Britain and received credit for discovering a side chamber near Cairo in 1926.
- [X] Academic liaison. At ease with scholars, officials and patrons. Explains his conclusions clearly.
- [~] Overseas field experience. Has worked on an organised Egyptian excavation. Experience away from an established dig is limited.
- [ ] Judgement under immediate threat. Untested.

### Personal assessment

Mr Pembroke is observant, personable and genuinely absorbed in his subject. He welcomes a well-founded objection and can explain why a small feature of a site deserves a closer look. His eagerness to return to Egypt is evident.

He is accustomed to expeditions with proper arrangements and clear lines of authority. In sudden difficulty he may look for someone else to take charge. He should travel with an experienced guide when working beyond established sites.

### Recommendation

SUITABLE — OFFER SPECIALIST APPOINTMENT

Engage Mr Pembroke to advise where the expedition should look, interpret what it uncovers and record the archaeological findings.
