### Personal assessment

Miss Ashford is composed, observant and discreet. She can put a person at ease while retaining a clear sense of what she needs to learn. She keeps her own counsel and may offer a conclusion before volunteering how she reached it.

She is unusually quick to piece together the object of an undertaking from a few apparently unrelated details. Give her only the information indispensable to the task at hand. Even routine details about who gave an instruction or when it was issued may tell her more than intended.

### Recommendation

SUITABLE — OFFER SPECIALIST APPOINTMENT WITH RESTRICTED BRIEFING

Engage Miss Ashford to advise on security, make discreet enquiries and assess outside interest in the expedition. Appoint a single liaison to issue instructions and receive her reports. All communication with the expedition leadership is to pass through that liaison.
