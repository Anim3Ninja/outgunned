EGYPTIAN EXPEDITION — PERSONNEL ASSESSMENT
Office of Sir Reginald Harcourt · Private and Confidential · 1934

Candidate:  Miss Shenanigans O’Lier
Age and nationality:  Approximately 30, American
Present occupation:  Expedition guide, based in a South American river port
Proposed appointment:  Expedition Guide and Field Scout

### Experience and requirements

- [X] Route finding. Experienced in choosing routes through difficult country and judging when a party should turn back.
- [X] Fieldcraft. Can track missing party members, assess terrain and keep a group moving when plans go awry.
- [X] Rescue under pressure. A photograph from the 1932 Amazon expedition supports the account of her recovering a man swept away during a river crossing.
- [X] Expedition work. Has guided archaeological parties and is accustomed to working with crews and porters.
- [~] Knowledge of the proposed route. Her strongest references are from South America. Local advice will be needed on water sources and current conditions in Egypt.

### Personal assessment

Miss O’Lier is self-assured, resourceful and willing to take responsibility when a party is in difficulty. She speaks plainly and is likely to object if someone presses her to take a route she considers unsafe. She takes the welfare of people in her charge seriously, even when it costs her time or puts her at risk.

### Recommendation

SUITABLE — OFFER SPECIALIST APPOINTMENT

Engage Miss O’Lier to scout routes and guide the party in the field. Give her a clear say in decisions about whether the ground ahead is safe to cross.
